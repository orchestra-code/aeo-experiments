## Key findings

- **When ChatGPT consults a site directly, it is usually not the brand being asked about.** Of 6,923 `site:`-scoped grounding searches evaluated in this study, 81% targeted third-party sites, 11% a tracked competitor of the brand whose prompts triggered them, and 8% the brand's own site.
- **The trusted-source list is institutional, not social.** Government and court sites receive 23% of third-party consultations; bar and professional associations another 9%. Reddit accounts for 0.14%. Re-weighting every client sector equally moves government to 22% — the institutional lean is a property of the model's behavior, not of our panel's industry mix.
- **Consulted domains are a measurably different population from cited-but-never-consulted domains.** Median Common Crawl graph rank #485,000 vs #2.3 million; 63% vs 43% ranked in the graph at all; median AI Placement Value Score 60 vs 44, with Premium+Strong tiers covering 62% vs 41%. The frozen models put the ranked-at-all odds ratio at [[OR_M1]] and the Premium/Strong odds ratio at [[OR_M3]].
- **Comparison prompts are the trigger.** 20% of brand-comparison answers included at least one direct consultation, versus 9% for brand-identity prompts and 8% for category-level prompts. The widespread assumption that asking about a brand sends AI to the brand's site is not what the data shows.
- The practical consequences: factual product-vs-competitor comparison pages are the content most directly tied to consultation behavior, and placements on directly-consulted publishers carry structural influence on AI answers.

## Why we asked

Since late July 2026, ChatGPT's grounding searches sometimes carry an explicit `site:domain.com` operator: the model electing to consult one specific site before, or instead of, searching the open web. The arrival of the behavior has been widely noted in the AI-visibility community. What had not been examined is the question that matters for anyone deciding where to spend content and PR effort: **who receives these consultations, and what do the chosen sites have in common?**

Our mechanistic prior: a direct consultation skips open-web ranking entirely, so the choice of site must come from the model's prior about who is authoritative — which predicts the consulted list should skew toward training-visible authority (web-graph centrality) rather than tracking current SERP strength alone.

## Methods in brief

We evaluated 97,377 grounding-search executions recorded for ChatGPT in this study, of which 6,923 carried a `site:` scope. Each scoped search was classified at read time against the triggering brand's current identity as *own*, *competitor*, or *third-party*; every consulted domain (2,934 including brand-panel domains) was additionally classified into a fourteen-kind taxonomy (AI-assisted, from the bare domain only, with the highest-volume domains reviewed by hand; the classification cache is committed for reproducibility).

For the authority contrast we built, per answer on category-level prompts, the pool of third-party domains the model either consulted directly or cited without consulting — 363,028 (answer, domain) pairs across 70,090 distinct domains evaluated in this study — and compared the two classes on Common Crawl harmonic-centrality rank and on the [AI Placement Value Score](https://www.spyglasses.io/en/docs/placements/ai-placement-value-score), computed with the production scorer. Cited-only AIPVS coverage comes from a seeded 5,000-domain random sample; sampling controls on the outcome side leaves odds ratios consistent and biases only the intercept.

The full pre-registration, including a disclosed pre-freeze predictor-selection step, is in the [frozen spec](https://github.com/orchestra-code/aeo-experiments) (commit `065e37d`). Because predictor selection examined the joint distributions before the freeze, we label the model results **exploratory with a disclosed selection step**, not clean confirmatory pre-registration.

## What we can and cannot claim

**This design measures a conditional association, not a cause.** Among domains the model retrieved or cited for category-level prompts, the ones it consults directly sit higher on public authority metrics. No one assigned authority at random; we cannot say that raising a domain's centrality or AIPVS causes consultations.

**The pool conditions on retrieval.** Every domain in the contrast was already surfaced by the model for that prompt. Domains the model never retrieves are invisible to this design, so nothing here describes the marginal probability that an arbitrary site gets consulted.

**The taxonomy is AI-assisted.** Domain kinds were classified by a language model from the bare domain name, with the highest-volume domains reviewed by hand and the full mapping committed. Residual classification error exists and predominantly blurs the boundary between the business categories, not the institutional ones.

**Panel mix shapes the raw shares.** Our clients skew toward certain industries; we publish the sector-balanced series alongside the observed one, and every headline claim above survives the balancing.

**The model results carry a disclosed selection step.** Predictors were chosen after examining joint distributions (pre-freeze), so the odds ratios are exploratory-with-disclosure rather than confirmatory. The direction and rough size of every reported effect were stable across the selection process.

Sample sizes throughout are counts evaluated in this study, not statements about the Spyglasses database.
